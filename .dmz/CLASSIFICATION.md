## Category key

- `agent-systems`: agent behavior, orchestration, memory, prompts,
  harnesses, and runtime control.
- `application-and-media`: application frameworks, UI, mobile, visual,
  animation, and media production.
- `business-domain`: operational workflows tied to a business function or
  regulated industry.
- `content-and-commercial`: writing, marketing, research, brand, growth,
  and commercial communication.
- `data-and-storage`: data extraction, databases, persistence, analytics,
  and data-intensive systems.
- `general-productivity`: skills that do not fit a narrower category.
- `infrastructure-and-operations`: deployment, systems, networking, cloud,
  terminals, and repository operations.
- `security-and-compliance`: defensive security, privacy, safety, and
  compliance controls.
- `software-engineering`: architecture, coding conventions, framework
  patterns, and design structure.
- `testing-and-quality`: tests, evaluation, benchmarks, regression,
  verification, and quality gates.
